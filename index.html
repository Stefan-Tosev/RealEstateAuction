The Process

Four steps. One fixed close date.

No open-ended offer chains. Every Auctio House sale runs on a published timeline, the same for every bidder.

  1. 01

    Register

    Submit proof of funds and identity verification. Approved bidders receive a paddle number and full due-diligence packet.

  2. 02

    Inspect

    Attend a private showing or virtual walkthrough. Title, survey, and inspection reports are published for every lot before bidding opens.

  3. 03

    Bid

    Bid live, in the open. Every offer and paddle number is visible on the lot page in real time — no sealed bids, no back-room offers.

  4. 04

    Close

    Highest bid past reserve wins at the gavel. Funds are released from escrow and closing is coordinated within 30 days.
